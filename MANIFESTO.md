# The Sūveryn Manifesto

*AI for work that can't leave the premises.*

Right now, confidentiality-bound work has two bad options: send sensitive data into someone else's closed cloud and hope, or sit out the biggest productivity shift in a generation. Neither is good enough.

Sūveryn is the third option: a secure, open application stack and virtual appliance, built on open-weight models and designed to run entirely on a customer's own premises, including in air-gapped environments.

We thrive on a seemingly paradoxical idea: radical openness in service of confidentiality.

We build in the open for three reasons:

1. **Security through openness.** Inspectable code, transparent dependencies, and no need to trust a black box blindly.
2. **Continuity.** The software remains available, even if Sūveryn Labs were to cease to exist.
3. **Collective progress.** A community that builds and improves the platform together.

Openness alone is not a moat. We compete on depth: playbooks and connectors built for the professions we serve, capabilities that generic open platforms don't provide out of the box.

We intend to share a meaningful part of our success with the community that helps build it. That means funding the Sūveryn Foundation and, eventually, giving the Foundation itself a stake in Sūveryn Labs.

If this is your kind of problem, the code is public. Come build it with us.
